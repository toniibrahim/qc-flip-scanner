- Demolition cost: $15K–$25K

- Total cost base: $614K–$724K
- ARV: $680K–$720K for a standard new-build bungalow in Saint-Hubert
- Net: −$4K to $106K depending on rebuild quality and cost control

At best, this is a break-even deal with massive execution risk. At a more realistic $250K rebuild, the net is deeply negative. The rule (§13): "renovation costs are realistic for Quebec contractor-market conditions" — $250K+ full rebuild for a $399K purchase does not create viable flip economics.

**Key risks:** Unknown demolition complications (foundation issues, contaminated soil, utility disconnections); full rebuild permits timeline (12–18 months minimum in Longueuil); contractor availability for full custom builds.
